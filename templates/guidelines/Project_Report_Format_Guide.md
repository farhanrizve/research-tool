### 6. Abstract
- 150-250 words
- Problem statement (1-2 sentences)
- Proposed solution (1-2 sentences)
- Methodology summary (1 sentence)
- Key outcomes (1-2 sentences)
- No citations or references

- Lists all chapters and sections

## Chapter 1: Introduction

### 1.1 Introduction

**Content Structure: 7-10 paragraphs**

#### Background (3-4 paragraphs)
- Global/context statistics and facts
- Current state of the problem area
- Relevant technological context
- Industry or domain overview

#### Problem Statement (2-3 paragraphs)
- Current challenges and limitations
- Inadequacies of existing solutions
- Specific gaps that need addressing
- Impact of the problem

#### Contribution (1-2 paragraphs)
- What the project adds to the field
- Novel aspects or improvements
- Significance of the contribution

#### Solution Offered (1-2 paragraphs)
- Overview of proposed system/solution
- Key features and capabilities
- How it addresses the identified problems

### 1.2 Objectives

**Content Structure: 2-3 objectives**

Format each objective as bullet points (▪ symbol):
```
▪ [Action verb] + [specific outcome]
   Brief explanation (1-2 sentences)
   
▪ [Action verb] + [specific outcome]
   Brief explanation (1-2 sentences)
```

**Example:**
```
1. Design and develop a crowdsourced missing person search platform
   that enables efficient reporting and tracking of missing individuals.
   
2. Implement real-time AI-based face recognition for automated
   matching of uploaded images with the missing person database.
```

### 1.3 Justification of Study

**Content Structure: 1-2 paragraphs**

- Why this study is necessary
- Relevance to local/global context
- Potential benefits and impact
- Alignment with industry needs or research gaps

### 1.4 Scope of Study

**Content Structure: 1-2 paragraphs with sub-sections**

- **Technology Stack:** Tools, frameworks, platforms used
- **Functional Boundaries:** What the system does
- **Geographical/Contextual Limits:** Target users, location, domain
- **Out of Scope:** Clearly stated limitations

## Chapter 2: Literature Review

### Chapter Description (1 paragraph)
- Overview of the review scope
- Number of sources reviewed
- Categorization of reviewed works
- Review organization explanation

### 2.1 Review of Relevant Literature

**Content Structure: 1 paragraph per paper**

For each reviewed paper, structure as:

```
[Paper Title] ([Year])
[Author(s)]

What was done: [Brief description of the study/paper]
How it was done: [Methodology or approach used]
Findings: [Key results or contributions]
Gaps/Limitations: [What the paper does not address or limitations]
Relevance to current study: [How this relates to your project]
```

**Guidelines:**
- Review minimum 8-12 relevant papers
- Organize by themes or chronological order
- Identify patterns and trends
- Highlight research gaps explicitly
- Connect gaps to your project's contribution

## References

**Format:** Use author-date format (not numbered inline)

**Sample Citation Style:**
```
Author(s) (Year) Title. Description or publication details.
```

**Guidelines:**
- Minimum 10-15 for undergraduate projects
- Include recent publications (last 5 years)
- Use academic sources (journals, conferences)
